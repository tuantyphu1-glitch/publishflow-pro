TIMELINE
  1-61    ride, crouch progressively (anticipation)
  62-90   pop (velocity break at 62), ollie pitch -10 deg, feet lift away from the board
  89-153  board flips 720 deg around its nose-tail axis (slow-in, fast middle, slow-out)
  146-160 feet come down, board rises -> catch at frame 160 (both feet on the deck)
  160-180 falling on the board, legs extend
  181     impact: all 4 wheels on the ground, velocity break
  181-216 knees absorb (compress 189, rebound 204, settle 216), keep rolling, smile
Air time 63-180 (3.9 s) = stylised hang time required by the timeline (real ~0.8 s).

KNOWN LIMITATIONS / NOTES
- Stylised hang time (3.9 s airborne) because the timeline puts 60 frames of flip in the air.
- During the flip the board is ~0.18 m below the soles (safety clearance so the rotating
  board never touches the shoes); could be reduced to ~0.12 m if a tighter look is wanted.
- Compared with the original design sheet: the sheet's legs are even longer (~half the
  body height), it has no separate shorts, and eyes/eyebrows are smaller/thinner.
  These were NOT changed because the brief asked to keep the v2 character.
- Rigid-part rig (no skinned mesh): elbows/knees are overlapping parts.
